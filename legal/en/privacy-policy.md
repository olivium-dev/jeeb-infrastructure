## 1. Who we are

Jeeb ("we", "us", "the Platform") is the data controller for personal data
processed in connection with Clients' and Jeebers' use of the Platform.
Contact: `privacy@jeeb.app` (placeholder). The Data Protection Officer can
be reached at the same address.

## 2. Personal data we process

We process the following categories of personal data:

| Category                  | Examples                                                                  | Source                |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Identity                  | Name, profile photo, date of birth, government ID (Jeebers)               | You                   |
| Contact                   | Mobile number, email                                                      | You                   |
| Authentication            | OTP history, device ID, login timestamps, IP address                      | Your device           |
| Location                  | Real-time GPS during a Request, pickup/drop-off addresses                 | Your device           |
| Transactional             | Request history, ratings, chat messages, voice notes within the Platform  | You + counterparty    |
| COD settlement            | Amount, currency, collection and reconciliation status, Jeeber payout destination details if supplied | Request parties / Jeeber |
| Compliance (Jeebers only) | Licence number, vehicle registration, background-check results            | You + vetting partner |
| Device & technical        | Device model, OS version, app version, crash logs                         | Your device           |

We do **not** intentionally collect sensitive categories (health, religion,
political opinion, biometric identifiers used for unique identification),
except where strictly necessary for safety verification (e.g. selfie match
to ID for Jeeber onboarding) and only with explicit consent.

## 3. Legal bases for processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases:

1. **Performance of a contract** — to provide the Platform, match Clients
   with Jeebers, record and reconcile cash-on-delivery, and handle support.
2. **Legal obligation** — tax records, KYC/AML, response to lawful requests
   from competent authorities.
3. **Legitimate interests** — fraud prevention, safety, service improvement,
   in-app analytics that do not profile individuals for advertising.
4. **Consent** — marketing communications, optional location collection
   outside an active Request, and any sensitive data we expressly request.
   Consent can be withdrawn at any time without affecting prior processing.

## 4. How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- create and authenticate accounts;
- match Clients with available Jeebers and route Requests;
- compute pricing, record cash collection, reconcile COD, and settle amounts owed to Jeebers;
- enable chat, voice, and rating between counterparties;
- enforce the Terms of Service and Prohibited Items Policy;
- prevent fraud, abuse, and unsafe behaviour;
- comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests;
- improve the service and develop new features (using aggregated and, where
  possible, pseudonymised data).

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do **not** use Request content
(pickup/drop-off, chat, voice) for advertising.

## 5. Sharing personal data

We share personal data only as follows:

| Recipient                              | Purpose                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| The counterparty to a Request           | Name and (limited) contact for delivery hand-off |
| Background-check and KYC providers      | Verify Jeeber eligibility                        |
| Cloud infrastructure providers          | Host and operate the Platform                    |
| Customer-support tooling                | Resolve in-app tickets                           |
| Law enforcement / regulators            | Where legally required                            |
| Acquirer in a corporate transaction     | Subject to equivalent protections                |

Each processor is bound by a written data-processing agreement that requires
equivalent technical and organisational measures and prohibits onward
transfer outside the agreed purpose.

## 6. International data transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the operating jurisdiction, we
rely on the transfer mechanism approved by the local regulator (e.g.
Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision). The list of transfer
destinations is available on request from `privacy@jeeb.app`.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to the applicable data-protection law, the User has the right to:

1. **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about them.
2. **Rectification** — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
3. **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — request deletion of their data,
   subject to legal retention obligations (see §8).
4. **Restriction** — restrict processing in defined circumstances.
5. **Portability** — receive data in a structured, machine-readable format.
6. **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
7. **Withdraw consent** — without affecting prior lawful processing.
8. **Complain** to the competent data-protection authority.

All rights can be exercised through the in-app **Privacy Centre** or by
emailing `privacy@jeeb.app`. We respond within 30 days; complex requests may
be extended once by a further 60 days with notice.

## 8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose for
which it was collected and to meet legal obligations:

| Data category                | Retention period                                          |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active account data          | Duration of the account                                   |
| Request history (operational) | 24 months after the last Request                          |
| COD settlement and tax records | As required by tax law (typically 7 years)              |
| Chat and voice messages      | 90 days from the end of the Request, then deleted         |
| KYC / background-check       | As required by AML regulation (typically 5 years)         |
| Crash and technical logs     | 90 days                                                   |
| Marketing-consent records    | Until consent is withdrawn + 12 months for audit          |

When a User deletes their account (in-app or by request), we delete personal
data within **30 days** except for records we are legally required to keep,
which are stored in restricted-access archives and not used for any other
purpose.

## 9. Security

We protect personal data with industry-standard technical and organisational
measures, including:

- TLS in transit and at-rest encryption for production data stores;
- principle-of-least-privilege role-based access control with audit logging;
- short-lived, rotated secrets — long-lived credentials are not used;
- segregated environments — production data is not used in development;
- mandatory background checks for staff with access to personal data;
- a documented incident-response plan with breach-notification timelines
  meeting the strictest applicable law (no later than 72 hours to the
  regulator and, where required, to affected Users).

No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but we
commit to acting in good faith, transparently, and quickly if an incident
occurs.

## 10. Children

The Platform is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly
collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete the data
without undue delay.

## 11. Cookies and analytics

The mobile app does not use third-party advertising cookies. We use a small
number of first-party analytics events (crash reporting, performance) that
do not build cross-app profiles.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

Material changes are announced in-app at least 14 days before they take
effect and trigger a re-consent flow where required.
