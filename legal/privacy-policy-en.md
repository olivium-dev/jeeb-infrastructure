## 4. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

- **Account management:** Create, authenticate, and manage User accounts.
- **Service delivery:** Match Clients with available Jeebers, route
  Requests, compute pricing, and facilitate delivery.
- **Payment processing:** Process Client payments, calculate Platform
  Commission, and settle Jeeber payouts.
- **Communication:** Enable in-app chat and voice between counterparties;
  send service-related notifications.
- **Safety and compliance:** Enforce the Terms of Service and Prohibited
  Items Policy; prevent fraud, abuse, and unsafe behaviour.
- **KYC and identity verification:** Verify Jeeber eligibility and
  identity through document and selfie verification.
- **Legal compliance:** Comply with Lebanese law, tax obligations, and
  respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.
- **Service improvement:** Analyse aggregated and, where possible,
  pseudonymised data to improve the Platform and develop new features.
- **Dispute resolution:** Investigate and resolve disputes between Users.

4.1 We do **not** sell personal data to any third party.

4.2 We do **not** use Request content (pickup/drop-off details, chat
messages, voice notes) for advertising or marketing purposes.

- Data required by law (tax records, KYC) is moved to restricted-access

- Archived data is not used for any purpose other than legal compliance.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the
purpose for which it was collected and to comply with legal obligations:

| Data Category                     | Retention Period                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active account data               | Duration of the active account                                                |
| Request history (operational)     | 24 months after the last Request, then anonymised or deleted                  |
| Payment and tax records           | As required by Lebanese tax law (currently 10 years per Article 107 of the Income Tax Law) |
| Chat and voice messages           | 90 days after the Request is completed, then permanently deleted              |
| KYC / identity documents          | Duration of the account + 5 years (per AML/KYC requirements)                 |
| Background-check results          | Duration of the active Jeeber account + 2 years                              |
| Location data (GPS traces)        | 12 months after the Request, then anonymised                                  |
| Crash and technical logs          | 90 days, then permanently deleted                                             |
| Marketing-consent records         | Until consent is withdrawn + 12 months for audit trail                        |
| IP addresses and login history    | 6 months                                                                      |

8.1 When a User deletes their account, we delete or anonymise personal
data within **30 calendar days**, except for records we are legally
required to retain, which are stored in restricted-access, encrypted
archives.

8.2 Anonymised data (from which the User can no longer be identified) may
be retained indefinitely for statistical and service-improvement purposes.

## 9. Data Security

We protect personal data with industry-standard technical and
organisational measures, including:

- **Encryption:** TLS 1.2+ for data in transit; AES-256 encryption for
  data at rest in production data stores.
- **Access control:** Principle of least privilege; role-based access
  control with multi-factor authentication for staff access to personal
  data; comprehensive audit logging.
- **Secrets management:** Short-lived, rotated secrets; no long-lived
  credentials in source code or configuration.
- **Environment segregation:** Production data is never used in
  development or testing environments.
- **Personnel:** Mandatory background checks and confidentiality
  agreements for all staff with access to personal data.
- **Incident response:** Documented incident-response plan with breach
  notification to the competent authority within **72 hours** and to
  affected Users without undue delay where the breach is likely to result
  in a high risk to their rights.

9.1 No system can guarantee absolute security. We commit to acting in good
faith, transparently, and promptly in the event of any data incident.
