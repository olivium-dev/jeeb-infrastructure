## 1. Scope and Applicability

1.1 This Policy applies to **every Request** published, accepted, or
fulfilled on the Jeeb Platform. Both the Client (who publishes the
Request) and the Jeeber (who accepts and fulfils it) share responsibility
for ensuring that no Request involves a Prohibited Item.

1.2 A Jeeber who, at the point of pickup or at any time during a delivery,
has reasonable grounds to believe the Request involves a Prohibited Item
**must immediately refuse the Request** and report it through the in-app
**Safety Centre**. Such refusal shall not count against the Jeeber's
rating, standing, or acceptance metrics.

1.3 Ignorance of this Policy does not constitute a defence. All Users are
deemed to have read and understood this Policy upon accepting the Terms of
Service.

## 2. Absolutely Prohibited Items (Zero Tolerance)

The following items **must never** be transported, delivered, handled, or
otherwise facilitated through the Platform under any circumstances.
Attempting to do so will result in **immediate permanent termination** of
the User's account and may be reported to Lebanese law enforcement
authorities.

### 2.1 Illegal Drugs and Controlled Substances

- Narcotics, psychotropic substances, and any controlled substance as
  defined under Lebanese Law No. 673/1998 and its amendments.
- Precursor chemicals used in the manufacture of controlled substances.
- Paraphernalia specifically designed for the use of illegal drugs.
- Exception: Prescription medication is addressed under Section 3.3
  (Restricted Items).

### 2.2 Weapons, Ammunition, and Explosives

- Firearms of any type (handguns, rifles, shotguns, automatic weapons).
- Ammunition, magazines, and firearm components.
- Military-grade weapons or equipment.
- Knives, swords, or bladed weapons carried or transported for use as
  weapons (utility knives in sealed retail packaging are permitted).
- Explosives, fireworks, blasting caps, detonators, and pyrotechnic
  devices.
- Tear gas, pepper spray, stun guns, tasers, or similar devices (where
  prohibited by Lebanese law).

### 2.3 Hazardous Materials

- Flammable liquids and gases: petrol, diesel (except in approved
  containers below 5 litres for emergency use), propane, butane canisters
  above small consumer-grade sizes (< 500g).
- Corrosive substances: strong acids (sulfuric, hydrochloric, nitric),
  strong bases (sodium hydroxide, potassium hydroxide).
- Radioactive materials of any kind.
- Asbestos and regulated hazardous waste.
- Compressed-gas cylinders (industrial grade).
- Toxic chemicals, poisons, and pesticides in quantities exceeding
  personal-use household amounts.
- Biological hazards: infectious materials, medical waste, used sharps.

### 2.4 Living Beings and Human Remains

- **Living animals** — unless Jeeb has launched a specific, approved
  pet-transport service with appropriate insurance, training, and
  veterinary oversight. No such service is currently active.
- **Human remains**, organs, blood, tissue, or other body parts — unless
  transported through an approved medical-courier flow with verified
  clinical paperwork from a licensed Lebanese medical institution.
- Any involvement in or facilitation of **human trafficking**.

### 2.5 Illegal or Harmful Content

- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM):** Any content depicting the
  sexual abuse or exploitation of a minor. Detection triggers immediate
  reporting to the Lebanese Internal Security Forces (ISF) and relevant
  international agencies under applicable law.
- Content that incites violence, terrorism, or genocide.
- Material promoting or facilitating terrorist activities.

### 2.6 Counterfeit and Fraudulent Items

- Counterfeit currency, coins, or financial instruments.
- Forged identification documents, passports, or official certificates.
- Counterfeit goods knowingly misrepresented as genuine (brand-name
  products with falsified trademarks).
- Stolen goods or goods reasonably suspected of being stolen.

### 2.7 Sanctioned or Embargoed Items

- Goods subject to international trade sanctions or embargoes applicable
  to Lebanon.
- Items whose import, export, or domestic transport is prohibited by
  Lebanese customs law.

## 3. Restricted Items (Allowed Under Strict Conditions)

The following items may be handled through the Platform **only** if the
specified conditions are fully met. The Client must declare the item type
at Request creation, and the Jeeber must explicitly acknowledge and accept
the restriction before proceeding.

### 3.1 Alcohol

- **Condition:** Legal in Lebanon. The Client must be of legal drinking
  age (18 years in Lebanon).
- The Jeeber must verify the recipient's age using a government-issued ID
  at the point of hand-off.
- Delivery to a recipient who appears visibly intoxicated is prohibited.
- Delivery is limited to commercially packaged, sealed alcohol products
  from licensed vendors.
- Quantity must not exceed amounts typical for personal consumption.

### 3.2 Tobacco, Vaping Products, and Shisha

- **Condition:** The recipient must be at least 18 years of age.
- The Jeeber must verify the recipient's age at hand-off.
- Quantities are limited to personal-use amounts as defined by Lebanese
  customs regulations.
- Loose or unpackaged tobacco is not permitted.

### 3.3 Prescription Medication

- The medication must be in its **original, sealed packaging** with the
  pharmacy label intact.
- A valid prescription matching the **recipient's legal name** must
  accompany the delivery (physical copy or verified digital prescription).
- The medication must **not** be a controlled substance under Lebanese
  Law No. 673/1998 (see Section 2.1).
- The pharmacy of origin must be a licensed Lebanese pharmacy.

### 3.4 Large Cash Sums

- Cash amounts exceeding the threshold set in the application (currently
  LBP equivalent of USD [X]) require:
  - Client identity verification through the Platform;
  - A Platform escrow arrangement;
  - Declaration of the purpose of the cash transfer.
- Cash amounts exceeding AML reporting thresholds must be declined and
  reported to the trust-and-safety team.

### 3.5 Fragile, Perishable, or Temperature-Sensitive Items

- Permitted, but at the **Client's risk**.
- The Client must declare the item as fragile, perishable, or
  temperature-sensitive at Request creation and acknowledge that the
  Platform's standard liability cap (Terms of Service, Section 10.3)
  applies.
- Jeeb assumes no special liability for spoilage, breakage, or
  temperature-related damage unless the Jeeber is found to have acted
  with gross negligence.

### 3.6 High-Value Items

- Items with a declared value above the high-value threshold set in the
  application (currently LBP equivalent of USD [X]) require:
  - A declared-value declaration at Request creation;
  - May attract a surcharge for enhanced handling.
- Without a declared-value declaration, loss or damage compensation is
  capped at the standard limit specified in the Terms of Service.

### 3.7 Documents and Official Papers

- Government documents, legal papers, notarised documents, and passports
  may be transported provided they belong to the Client or the Client has
  lawful authority to transport them.
- The Jeeber must obtain a signature or photo confirmation upon delivery.

## 4. Reporting Suspected Violations

4.1 **How to report.** Either party may report a suspected violation
through:
- The in-app **Safety Centre** (available on every active Request screen);
- Email to safety@jeeb.app;
- In-app chat support with an urgent safety flag.

4.2 **Review timeline.**
- **Safety-of-life reports** (weapons, trafficking, CSAM): Reviewed
  **immediately** by the trust-and-safety team, 24/7.
- **Standard reports:** Reviewed within **24 hours** during business days.

4.3 **Cooperation with authorities.** Jeeb cooperates with the Lebanese
Internal Security Forces (ISF), General Security, Lebanese Customs, and
other competent authorities in response to lawful, validly served requests.
We do not pre-emptively share User data beyond what is required by law.

4.4 **Reporter protection.** Users who report suspected violations in
good faith are protected from retaliation. False or malicious reports may
themselves result in account action.

## 5. Enforcement and Consequences

5.1 Violations are enforced on a graduated scale appropriate to the
severity of the violation:

| Severity         | Examples                                                                           | Action                                                     |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Minor**        | First-time accidental violation of Section 3 conditions (e.g., missing age check)  | Written warning + mandatory acknowledgement                |
| **Moderate**     | Repeat Section 3 violation; failure to declare restricted items                    | Temporary suspension (7–30 days) + mandatory re-training   |
| **Severe**       | Any Section 2 violation                                                            | Immediate permanent termination + report to authorities    |
| **Criminal**     | Section 2.4 (trafficking), Section 2.5 (CSAM), or any offence reportable by law    | Permanent termination + law enforcement report + evidence preservation |

5.2 Jeeb reserves the right to escalate any violation to law enforcement
where required by law or where public safety is at risk.

5.3 Jeeb may, at its sole discretion, permanently ban a User after a
single severe violation without prior warning.

## 6. Appeals

6.1 A User whose account is suspended or terminated under this Policy may
appeal through:
- The in-app **Appeals** flow;
- Email to appeals@jeeb.app;

within **30 calendar days** of the action.

6.2 Appeals are reviewed by a member of the trust-and-safety team who was
**not** involved in the original decision.

6.3 The decision on appeal is **final**, unless the User presents
materially new evidence not available at the time of the original decision.

6.4 Severe and criminal-level enforcement actions (Section 2 violations)
are generally **not eligible** for appeal, except in cases of verified
mistaken identity or demonstrable Platform error.

## 7. Jeeber Duty to Inspect and Refuse

7.1 The Jeeber has a **duty to inspect** items at the point of pickup
where reasonably practicable. This does not require the Jeeber to open
sealed packages but does require the Jeeber to exercise reasonable
judgment based on:
- Visual inspection of the item's exterior;
- Weight, shape, or smell that suggests a Prohibited Item;
- The Client's behaviour or statements;
- Any other circumstances that would raise concern to a reasonable person.

7.2 If the Jeeber suspects a Prohibited Item, the Jeeber must:
- Refuse to proceed with the delivery;
- Report through the in-app Safety Centre;
- Not confront the Client or attempt to investigate further.

7.3 A Jeeber who knowingly transports a Prohibited Item is subject to the
same enforcement actions as the Client who requested it, including
permanent termination and law enforcement referral.

## 8. Updates to This Policy

8.1 Material changes to this Policy follow the same in-app notice and
re-consent flow as the Terms of Service (14 calendar days' prior notice
for material changes).

8.2 Updates to the restricted items list (Section 3) that **expand**
restrictions may be made with shorter notice to comply with new Lebanese
regulations. Such updates do not require re-consent if they solely add
restrictions.

8.3 Updates that **reduce** restrictions (i.e., newly permit previously
restricted items) do not require re-consent.
