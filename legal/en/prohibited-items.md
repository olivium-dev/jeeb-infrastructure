> **Draft notice.** This Policy is incorporated by reference into the Terms
> of Service. Breach is a material breach of the Terms and grounds for
> immediate suspension. Some items in this Policy may additionally
> constitute criminal offences in the operating jurisdiction.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to every Request published or accepted on the Platform.
Both the Client (who publishes the Request) and the Jeeber (who accepts it)
are responsible for ensuring the Request does not involve a Prohibited Item.

A Jeeber who, at the point of pickup, has reasonable grounds to believe the
Request involves a Prohibited Item **must refuse the Request** and report
it through the in-app safety flow. Refusal on these grounds will not count
against the Jeeber's standing.

## 2. Absolutely prohibited (zero tolerance)

The following items must not be transported, delivered, or otherwise handled
through the Platform under any circumstances. Attempting to do so will
result in immediate, permanent termination and may be reported to law
enforcement.

### 2.1 Illegal goods

- Narcotics, psychotropic substances, and any controlled substance not
  accompanied by a valid prescription and authorised pharmacy paperwork.
- Counterfeit currency, counterfeit documents, forged identification.
- Stolen goods or goods reasonably suspected of being stolen.

### 2.2 Weapons and explosives

- Firearms, ammunition, components of firearms.
- Knives or bladed weapons sold or carried for use as a weapon.
- Explosives, fireworks, blasting caps, detonators.
- Tear gas, pepper spray (where prohibited by local law), stun devices.

### 2.3 Hazardous materials

- Flammable liquids and gases (petrol, propane, butane canisters above
  small consumer sizes).
- Corrosives, strong acids, strong bases.
- Radioactive materials.
- Asbestos and other regulated hazardous waste.
- Compressed-gas cylinders.

### 2.4 Living beings and human remains

- Living animals, except where Jeeb has launched a specific approved pet
  service with appropriate insurance and training.
- Human remains, organs, blood, or other body parts, except via the
  approved medical-courier flow with verified clinical paperwork.
- Trafficking of any person.

### 2.5 Child sexual abuse material and other unlawful content

- Any content depicting the sexual abuse or exploitation of a minor.
  Detection triggers immediate reporting to law enforcement under
  applicable law.
- Content that incites violence, terrorism, or genocide.

## 3. Restricted (allowed only under strict conditions)

The following may be handled only if the listed conditions are met. The
Client must declare the item at Request creation and the Jeeber must
explicitly accept the restriction.

### 3.1 Alcohol (where legal in the operating jurisdiction)

- The Client must be of legal drinking age.
- The Jeeber must verify the recipient's government-issued ID at hand-off.
- Cannot be delivered to a recipient who appears intoxicated.

### 3.2 Tobacco, vaping products, shisha

- Recipient must be of legal age.
- Quantity limited to personal-use amounts.

### 3.3 Prescription medication

- Original packaging, intact, with a prescription matching the recipient's
  legal name.
- Must not be a controlled substance per §2.1.

### 3.4 Large cash sums

- Cash above the threshold set in the app requires Client identity
  verification and a Platform escrow arrangement; otherwise refused.

### 3.5 Fragile, perishable, or temperature-sensitive items

- Permitted but at the Client's risk: the Client must declare the item and
  acknowledge that the Platform's general liability cap (Terms §9.3)
  applies.

### 3.6 High-value items

- Items above the high-value threshold (set in the app) require a
  declared-value sticker and may attract a surcharge. Without declaration,
  loss/damage compensation is capped at the standard limit.

## 4. Reporting and enforcement

4.1 Either party may report a suspected violation through the in-app
**Safety Centre**. Reports are reviewed by the trust-and-safety team within
24 hours; safety-of-life reports are reviewed immediately.

4.2 Jeeb cooperates with law enforcement in response to lawful, validly
served requests. We do not pre-emptively share content beyond what is
required by law.

4.3 Enforcement actions follow a graduated scale appropriate to the
violation:

| Severity        | Examples                                                | Action                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Minor           | First-time accidental violation of §3 conditions        | Warning + acknowledgement                    |
| Moderate        | Repeat §3 breach                                        | Temporary suspension (7–30 days)             |
| Severe          | Any §2 breach                                           | Immediate permanent termination + report     |
| Criminal        | §2.5 or any breach reportable to authorities by law     | Permanent termination + law-enforcement report |

## 5. Appeals

5.1 A User whose account is suspended or terminated under this Policy may
appeal through the in-app **Appeals** flow within 30 days of the action.

5.2 Appeals are reviewed by a member of trust-and-safety who was not
involved in the original decision. Decisions on appeal are final unless
new evidence is presented.

## 6. Updates

Material changes to this Policy follow the same in-app notice and re-consent
flow as the Terms of Service. The list of restricted items in §3 may be
updated more frequently to reflect local regulation; such updates do not
require re-consent if they reduce restrictions.
